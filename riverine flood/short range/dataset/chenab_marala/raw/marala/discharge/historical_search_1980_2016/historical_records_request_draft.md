# Draft request: historical observed Chenab discharge at Marala, 1980-2016

Prepared 30 October 2026. **Not sent.** Use the relevant agency's official contact route; no recipient address is assumed.

Suggested custodians: IRSA historical data/MIS-GIS team; WAPDA Surface Water Hydrology/Hydrology and Water Management; Executive Engineer, Marala Barrage Division, Punjab Irrigation Department. Send as separate requests where appropriate.

Subject: Research request for original daily Marala upstream discharge, 1 January 1980-31 December 2016

We are preparing a research model for ten-day daily streamflow forecasting at Marala Headworks on the Chenab River, Pakistan. We request the historical **observed upstream/inflow daily discharge series** for 1 January 1980 through 31 December 2016, including flood and non-flood periods, preferably as CSV or Excel.

If you hold only part of that period, please provide the available years and identify any missing dates. We already have WAPDA's public dated Marala inflow series starting 1 March 2016; the main missing interval is 1 January 1980 through 29 February 2016.

For IRSA, please consider the historical daily mean upstream discharge held in the WCAP MIS/GIS database described in section 2.8.1 and Table 2-44 of *Improvement of Water Resources Management of Indus Basin to Enhance the Capacity of Indus River System Authority*, Volume I. For Punjab Irrigation, please advise on the older daily records held by Marala Barrage Division. These documentary references are attached as evidence of the datasets sought, not as a claim that public redistribution is already authorized.

Please provide downstream/outflow, diversions and stage records **as separate variables** where available; do not substitute downstream release for upstream inflow. Original hourly/subdaily records are also welcome, with their native timestamps.

Please include or confirm:

- Station identifier, coordinates, gauge location changes and rating-curve changes.
- Units and whether each value is a daily mean, instantaneous reading, daily maximum or another statistic.
- Observation time/timezone or daily averaging-period bounds, and the meaning of the date label.
- Missing-value codes, quality flags, revisions, estimated/interpolated values, measurement-method changes and any transformations already applied.
- Original observation and publication/availability times where retained, to support a forecast evaluation that respects information available at issuance.
- Citation, license, redistribution conditions, application procedure and any applicable fees.
- Coverage specifically for October 2014-February 2016, so earlier records can be reconciled with the existing March 2016 onward extract.

We will preserve original values, retain provenance and quality flags, and keep missing dates explicit. Estimated or simulated discharge will not be labeled as observed ground truth.

If the requested historical data are held by another office, please identify the responsible custodian and the appropriate request process.
