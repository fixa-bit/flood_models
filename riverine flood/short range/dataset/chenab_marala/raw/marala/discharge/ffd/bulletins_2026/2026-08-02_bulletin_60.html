RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I. METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • Yesterday’s westerly wave over northeast Iran, today lies over north Afghanistan.

  • Yesterday’s well marked low over southeast Rajasthan (India), today lies over central Rajasthan (India) with central pressure of 993 hPa and is likely to move slightly northeastwards towards northeast Rajasthan (India) during next 24-hours.

  • Seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northwest Balochistan.

  • Moderate moist currents from Arabian sea and Bay of Bengal (along the foot hills of Himalayan mountains) are penetrating into central and upper parts of the country up to 5,000 feet.

II. METEOROLOGICAL OUTLOOK              

  • Under the influence of well-marked low-pressure system, currently lying over central Rajasthan (India) with moderate supporting monsoon currents, a wet spell in which isolated heavy falls are expected over Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, eastern parts of Sindh and eastern Balochistan up till 4th August. 

III. HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST

  • River Indus at Sukkur is in Medium flood level at upstream.

  • River Indus at Kalabagh, Chashma, Guddu and Sukkur is in Low flood level at downstream.

  • River Ravi at Balloki is in Low flood level at upstream.

IV. HYDROLOGICAL OUTLOOK

  • River Indus at Sukkur will remain in Low flood level at upstream during next 24-hours.

       WARNING (Up till 06th August): 

  • River Chenab at Marala, Khanki and Qadirabad is expected to fluctuate between Low to Medium flood level during the period.

  • Medium to High level flows are expected in nullahs of Jhelum, Chenab (Dora, Dotta, Dowara, Bhimber, Halsi, Aik and Palkhu) and Ravi (Bein, Bassanter, Deg and Sakki). 

  • Flash flooding is expected in the hill torrents of DG Khan, the streams of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, river Kabul and its associated tributaries, and northern Balochistan.

  • Urban Flooding is also expected in Rawalpindi, Jhelum, Gujranwala, Sargodha, Mandi Bahauddin, Sialkot, Gujrat, Narowal, Lahore and Faisalabad. 

  • Urban flooding, water logging and prolonged water ponding is expected in Badin, Hyderabad, Jamshoro, Khairpur, Matiari, Mirpur Khas, Sanghar, Shaheed Benazir Abad, Sujawal, Tando Allahyar, Tando Muhammad Khan, Tharparkar, Thatta and Umer Kot during next 24 hours.

5. HOW CLOSE IS EACH RIVER TO ITS LIMIT?

Balloki is in medium flood at 55.1 — 9.9 below its medium flood level (65.0). Each bar is one station’s own flood levels across its maximum capacity; the solid run is the flow so far, the pale run is the room still left.

All figures in thousands of cusecs. Full bar = maximum (design) capacity. Solid colour is the flow reached so far and the pale wash beside it is the room still left; the dark line between them is the current reading, printed beneath it. Thin white lines are that station’s flood levels with their thresholds beneath, so the same colour can begin at a different point on every bar. Each band is named inside itself — initialled (BL, L, M, H, VH, EH) where the band is too narrow for the words, and left blank where it is too narrow for those. Stations without a design capacity are not shown.
