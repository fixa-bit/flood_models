RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I. METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • Weak trough of westerly wave lies over northern parts of the country. 

  • The low-pressure system over central parts of Uttar Pradesh (India) persists with central pressure of 997 hPa.

  • Seasonal low-pressure area lies over northeast Balochistan.

  • Weak moist currents from Arabian sea and Bay of Bengal are penetrating into central and upper parts of the country up to 3,000 feet.

II. METEOROLOGICAL OUTLOOK              

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over upper catchments of eastern rivers including Islamabad, Kashmir, northeast & south Punjab during next 24-hours. 

III. HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST

  • River Indus at Kalabagh and Chashma is in Low flood level at downstream.

  • All other major rivers are flowing in their normal flows at other gauging stations.

 

IV. HYDROLOGICAL OUTLOOK

  • No significant change.

5. HOW CLOSE IS EACH RIVER TO ITS LIMIT?

Kalabagh is in low flood at 346.4 — 28.6 below its medium flood level (375.0). Each bar is one station’s own flood levels across its maximum capacity; the solid run is the flow so far, the pale run is the room still left.

All figures in thousands of cusecs. Full bar = maximum (design) capacity. Solid colour is the flow reached so far and the pale wash beside it is the room still left; the dark line between them is the current reading, printed beneath it. Thin white lines are that station’s flood levels with their thresholds beneath, so the same colour can begin at a different point on every bar. Each band is named inside itself — initialled (BL, L, M, H, VH, EH) where the band is too narrow for the words, and left blank where it is too narrow for those. Stations without a design capacity are not shown.
