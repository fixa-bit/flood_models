DAILY FLOOD BULLETIN

  1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I:    HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK            

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows.

 

     II:  METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • Yesterday’s shallow trough of westerly wave over northeastern parts of the country has moved eastwards.

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over North Balochistan.

  • Moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into central parts of the country up to 3000 feet.

  • A low-pressure area over West Bengal (India) with central pressure 999 hPa is expected to move northwestwards. 

 

III:  WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over upper catchments of rivers Indus and Jhelum, Islamabad and Kashmir. 

    Punjab: Rawalpindi, Attock, Bhakkar, Chiniot, Dera Ghazi Khan, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Hafizabad, Jhang, Kasur, Khushab, Lahore, Layyah, Mandi Bahauddin, Nankana Sahib, Okara, Rajanpur, Sargodha, Sheikhupura and Toba Tek Singh. 

Balochistan: Barkhan, Musakhel, Sherani and Zhob.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Abbottabad, Batagram, Buner, DI Khan, Haripur, Kohistan, Mansehra, Mardan, Shangla, Swabi, Swat and Dir. 

 

IV: WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS 

Wet spell is likely to decrease.

 

  1. WEEKLY WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR RIVER’S CATCHMENTS

(16th- July, 2026 to 22nd- July, 2026)

 

  • RAINFALL OUTLOOK:

    A strong monsoon activity is expected to commence over upper catchments of all major rivers including Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit Baltistan, Kashmir and upper Punjab from 20th July 2026. 

     

  • FLOOD SITUATION IN MAJOR RIVERS:

    River flows are expected to increase moderately in all the eastern rivers due to the Expected precipitation from 20 July 2026 onward. However, flows in the Chenab, Ravi and Sutlej rivers will depend on releases from upstream Indian reservoir.
