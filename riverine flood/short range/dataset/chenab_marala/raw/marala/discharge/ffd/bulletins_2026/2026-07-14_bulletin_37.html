DAILY FLOOD BULLETIN

  1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I:    HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK            

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows.

 

     II:  METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • Shallow trough of westerly wave lies over northeastern parts of the country.

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northeast Balochistan.

  • Moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into central parts of the country up to 3000 feet.

  • A low-pressure area has developed with central pressure 999 hPa over West Bengal (India).

 

III:  WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over upper catchments of all the major rivers, Islamabad, Gilgit Baltistan and Kashmir.

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Abbottabad, Bajaur, Bannu, Batagram, Buner, Charsadda, Chitral, D. I. Khan, Hangu, Haripur, Karak, Khyber, Kohat, Kohistan, Kolai Palas, Kurram, Lakki Marwat, Dir, Malakand, Mansehra, Mardan, Mohmand, Waziristan, Nowshera, Orakzai, Peshawar, Shangla, Swabi, Swat and Tank. 

    Punjab: Rawalpindi, Attock, Chakwal, Jhelum, Bahawalnagar, Bhakkar, Chiniot, Dera Ghazi Khan, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Gujrat, Hafizabad, Jhang, Kasur, Khanewal, Khushab, Lahore, Layyah, Mandi Bahauddin, Mianwali, Muzaffargarh, Nankana Sahib, Narowal, Okara, Pakpattan, Rajanpur, Sahiwal, Sargodha, Sheikhupura, Sialkot, Toba Tek Singh and Vehari. 

    Balochistan: Barkhan, Duki, Khuzdar, Musakhel, Sherani and Zhob. 

    Sindh: Dadu, Qamber-Shahdadkot and Larkana. 

  • Isolated heavy falls is also expected at Islamabad, Central Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Northwest Punjab, and DG Khan. 

 

IV: WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS 

Wet spell is likely to continue with less intensity.

 

  1. WEEKLY WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR RIVER’S CATCHMENTS

(15th- July, 2026 to 21st- July, 2026)

 

  • RAINFALL OUTLOOK:

    A strong monsoon activity is expected to commence over upper catchments of all major rivers including Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit Baltistan, Kashmir and upper Punjab from 20th July 2026. 

     

  • FLOOD SITUATION IN MAJOR RIVERS:

    River flows are also expected to increase from 20th July 2026.
