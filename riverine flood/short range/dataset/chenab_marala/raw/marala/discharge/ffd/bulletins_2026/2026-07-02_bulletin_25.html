DAILY FLOOD BULLETIN

  1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I:    HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK            

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows.

  • Flash flooding is expected in the tributaries of Kabul River, hill torrents of DG Khan, Rajanpur and Northeast Balochistan during next 24 hours.

     II:  METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • The trough associated with yesterday's westerly wave continues to persist over the northwestern parts of Pakistan.

  • The seasonal low-pressure area remains positioned over northwest Balochistan.

  •  Moist currents are penetrating the upper parts of the country, extending up to an altitude of 3,000 feet

III:  WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Widespread rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over upper catchments of all the major rivers including Islamabad, Gilgit Baltistan, Kashmir and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa with isolated heavy falls in Chitral, Upper Dir, Lower Dir, Swat, Shangla, Buner, Malakand, Bajaur,Upper Kohistan, Lower Kohistan, Kolai Pallas Kohistan, Mansehra, Torghar, Battagram, Abbottabad, Haripur, Mardan, Swabi, Charsadda, Peshawar, Nowshera, Punjab: Rawalpindi, Attock, Jhelum, Chakwal, Sargodha, Khushab, Mianwali, Gujranwala, Hafizabad, Gujrat, Mandi Bahaudin, Sialkot, Narowal, DG Khan and Rajanpur Balochistan: Zhob, Kalat, Khuzdar.

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Khyber, Mohmand, Kohat, Hangu, Karak, Orakzai, Kurram, Bannu, Lakki Marwat, North Waziristan, Dera Ismail Khan, Tank, South Waziristan, Punjab: Lahore, Kasur, Sheikhupura, Nankana Sahib, Faisalabad, Chiniot, Toba Tek Singh, Okara, Sahiwal, Pakpattan, Bahawalnagar, Multan, Muzaffargarh, Lodhran, Vehari, Khanewal.

Balochistan: Quetta, Loralai, Barkhan, Musa Khel, Killa Saifullah, Sheerani, Ziarat, Awaran, Panjgur, Washuk and Kohlu. 

IV: WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS 

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm with is expected over upper catchments of all the major rivers including Islamabad, Gilgit Baltistan, Kashmir, Upper Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab: Rawalpindi, Attock, Jhelum, Chakwal, Sargodha, Khushab, Mianwali, Gujranwala, Hafizabad, Gujrat, Mandi Bahaudin, Sialkot, Narowal, Lahore, Kasur, Sheikhupura, Nankana Sahib, Faisalabad, DG Khan, Rajanpur.

Balochistan: Zhob, Loralai, Barkhan, Musa Khel, Killa Saifullah, Sheerani, Ziarat, Kalat, Khuzdar. 

  1. WEEKLY SIGNIFICANT WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR RIVER’S CATCHMENTS

(03rd- July, 2026 to 09th- July, 2026)

  • RAINFALL OUTLOOK:

    Under the influence of a westerly wave and moisture incursion from the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal, along the Himalayan foothills, Isolated to Scattered monsoon rains with isolated heavy falls are expected over the upper catchments of the all major rivers, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, north-northeast Punjab, and north Balochistan, from 03rd July to 05th July.

  • FLOOD SITUATION IN MAJOR RIVERS:

Flows are expected to increase in all the major rivers.
