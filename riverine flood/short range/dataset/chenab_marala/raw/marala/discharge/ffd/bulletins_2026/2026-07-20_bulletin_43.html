RAINFALL FORECAST — NEXT 24 & 48 HOURS

1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I: HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK

  • River Chenab at Khanki and Qadirabad are in Low flood level. All other major rivers are in their normal flow.

  • WARNING: On account of  prevailing westerly wave coupled with strong monsoon incursion from Arabian sea and Bay of Bengal over the catchments of all major rivers, flows are likely to increase significantly till 24th July. 

    • High Flood level is expected in River Chenab at Marala, Jhelum at upstream Mangla and associated nullahs of Jhelum, Chenab (Dora, Dotta, Dowara, Bhimber, Halsi, Aik and Palkhu) and Ravi (Bein, Bassanter, Deg and Sakki). 

    • High flows are also expected in river Kabul and its associated tributaries.

    • Flash flooding is expected in the hill torrents of DG Khan, the streams of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and northern Balochistan.

    • Urban Flooding is also expected in Rawalpindi, Jhelum, Gujranwala, Sargodha, Khushab, Sialkot, Gujrat, Narowal, Lahore, Okara, Sahiwal, Bahawalnagar and Faisalabad. 

II: METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES

  • Yesterday’s trough of westerly wave over Afghanistan today lies over northern parts of the country.

  • Monsoon trough extends from northeastern parts of the country towards northern parts of India along the foot hills of Himalayas.

  • Yesterday's low-pressure area over northwest Jharkhand (India) today lies at south Utter Pradesh (India).

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northwest Balochistan.

  • Moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into the central parts and upper parts of the country up to 5000 feet.

III: WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Scattered to widespread rain-wind/thunderstorm with isolated heavy to very heavy falls is expected over upper catchments of all major rivers, Islamabad, Kashmir, Gilgit Baltistan 

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: DI Khan, Haripur, Karak, Kohat, Lakki Marwat, Nowshera, Swabi and Tank.

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm with isolated heavy falls is expected over

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Abbottabad, Bajaur, Bannu, Batagram, Buner, Charsadda, Hangu, Khyber, Kohistan, Kurram, Malakand, Mansehra, Mardan, Mohmand, Waziristan, Orakzai, Peshawar, Shangla, Swat, Tor Ghar and Dir. 

    Punjab: DG Khan, Layyah, Multan, Muzaffargarh, Sahiwal, Okara, Pakpattan, Bahawalnagar and Rajanpur.
