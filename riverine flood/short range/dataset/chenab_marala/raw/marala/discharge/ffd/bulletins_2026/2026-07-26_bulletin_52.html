RAINFALL FORECAST — NEXT 24 & 48 HOURS

1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I: HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK

  • River Indus at Kalabagh, Chashma and Taunsa, river Chenab at Marala, Khanki, Qadirabad, Chiniot Bridge and Trimmu, river Ravi at Balloki and Sidhnai are in Low flood level.

  • All major rivers at other gauging stations are flowing in their normal flows.

II: METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES

  • Yesterday’s trough of westerly wave over northwest parts of the country, today lies over northeastern parts of country.

  • Yesterday's low-pressure area over southwest Rajasthan (India) and adjoining Gujarat, today lies over west Rajasthan (India) and has weakened. 

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northwest Balochistan.

  • Weak moist currents from Bay of Bengal are penetrating into the country up to 5,000 feet.

  • Yesterday’s low pressure over south of West Bengal (India) and adjoining Orissa (India) persists.

5. HOW CLOSE IS EACH RIVER TO ITS LIMIT?

Sidhnai is in medium to high flood at 60.1 — 29.9 below its very high flood level (90.0). Each bar is one station’s own flood levels across its maximum capacity; the solid run is the flow so far, the pale run is the room still left.

All figures in thousands of cusecs. Full bar = maximum (design) capacity. Solid colour is the flow reached so far and the pale wash beside it is the room still left; the dark line between them is the current reading, printed beneath it. Thin white lines are that station’s flood levels with their thresholds beneath, so the same colour can begin at a different point on every bar. Each band is named inside itself — initialled (BL, L, M, H, VH, EH) where the band is too narrow for the words, and left blank where it is too narrow for those. Stations without a design capacity are not shown.
