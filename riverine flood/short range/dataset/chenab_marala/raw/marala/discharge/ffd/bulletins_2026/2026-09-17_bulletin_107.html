RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I.      METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • A trough of westerly wave lies over the central and upper parts of the country. 

  • Yesterday's low pressure system over Arabian Sea along coast of Gujarat has dissipated and become insignificant. 

  • Seasonal low-pressure area lies over west Balochistan.

  • Moderate moist currents from Arabian Sea are penetrating into the upper and central parts of the country up to 3,000 feet.

II.     METEOROLOGICAL OUTLOOK              

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the catchments of all the major rivers, Islamabad, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Kashmir, eastern Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan and Punjab during next 24-hours.

III.    HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST

  • All major rivers are flowing in their normal flows at all gauging stations.

5. HOW CLOSE IS EACH RIVER TO ITS LIMIT?

Balloki is in below low to low flood at 39.2 — 0.8 below its low flood level (40.0). Each bar is one station’s own flood levels across its maximum capacity; the solid run is the flow so far, the pale run is the room still left.

All figures in thousands of cusecs. Full bar = maximum (design) capacity. Solid colour is the flow reached so far and the pale wash beside it is the room still left; the dark line between them is the current reading, printed beneath it. Thin white lines are that station’s flood levels with their thresholds beneath, so the same colour can begin at a different point on every bar. Each band is named inside itself — initialled (BL, L, M, H, VH, EH) where the band is too narrow for the words, and left blank where it is too narrow for those. Stations without a design capacity are not shown.
