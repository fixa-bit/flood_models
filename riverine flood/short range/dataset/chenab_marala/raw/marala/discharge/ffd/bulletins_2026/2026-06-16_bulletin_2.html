SUBJECT: DAILY FLOOD BULLETIN 

 

RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

 

I:    HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK            

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows.

  • Flows may also increase in the hill torrents of DG khan and nullahs of Northeast Balochistan with in next 72 hours. 

 

     II:   METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES  

  1. Yesterday’s trough of westerly wave is affecting upper and central parts of the country. 

  2. Moist currents from the Arabian Sea are penetrating into the southern and central parts of the country up to 3000ft. 

  3. Seasonal low lies over West Balochistan and adjoining areas of Afghanistan. 

III: WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

Moderate rain-thunderstorms are likely at isolated places over the upper catchments of the Indus, Jhelum, and Chenab rivers, along with the Peshawar and Kohat divisions. Scattered dust/windstorms and rain-thunderstorms are expected over Islamabad, Kashmir, Rawalpindi, Gujrat, Gujranwala, Lahore, Sargodha, Faisalabad, Sahiwal, Multan, Dera Ghazi Khan, and Bahawalpur divisions. Moderate rain-thunderstorms at isolated places are also likely over Northeastern Balochistan and upper & central Sindh.

IV: WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 72-HOURS 

Scattered rain-thunderstorms of moderate intensity, with isolated heavy rainfall and hailstorms, are expected over Islamabad, Kashmir, Gilgit-Baltistan, Punjab, Northern Balochistan, as well as the Peshawar, Kohat, Bannu, and Dera Ismail Khan divisions.

  • RAINFALL OUTLOOK:

No flood generating rainfall is expected over upper catchments of all major rivers. 

  • FLOOD SITUATION IN MAJOR RIVERS:

No flood situation is expected.
