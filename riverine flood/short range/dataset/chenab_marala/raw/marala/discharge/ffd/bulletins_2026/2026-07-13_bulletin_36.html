DAILY FLOOD BULLETIN

  1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I:    HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK            

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows.

 

     II:  METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • Weak trough of westerly wave over northern parts of the country has moved slightly eastwards.

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northeast Balochistan with central pressure 991hPa.

  • Moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into the country up to 3000 feet.
