DAILY FLOOD BULLETIN

  1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I:    HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK            

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows.

WATCH: On account of approaching westerly wave coupled with strong monsoon incursion expected to commence from 20th July over the catchments of all major rivers, consequently river flows are expected to increase significantly along with the nullahs of river Ravi and Chenab, including the hill torrents of Dera Ghazi Khan, streams of KPK and northern Balochistan.

 

     II:  METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • A fresh trough of westerly wave lies over Northwest of Iran.

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northwest Balochistan.

  • Weak moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into central parts of the country up to 3000 feet.

  • Yesterday's low-pressure area over West Bengal (India), with a central pressure of 999 hPa persists at the same location and is expected to move northwestwards & intensify during next 24-36 hours.

III:  WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over upper catchments of rivers Jhelum, Chenab and Ravi, Islamabad and Kashmir. 

    Punjab: Rawalpindi, Lahore, Narowal, Sialkot, Gujranwala, Sheikhupura and DG Khan. 

Balochistan: Khuzdar, Kalat and Musakhel.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Batagram, Chitral, Kohistan, Mansehra, Swat and Dir. 

 

IV: WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS 

Rainfall activity is likely to decrease.

 

  1. WEEKLY SIGNIFICANT WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR RIVER’S CATCHMENTS

(17th- July, 2026 to 23rd- July, 2026)

 

  • RAINFALL OUTLOOK:

    WATCH: Widespread thunderstorm/rain with isolated heavy to very heavy falls/torrential rain is expected over upper catchments of all the major rivers along with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit Baltistan, Kashmir and upper Punjab from 20th July 2026. 

     

  • FLOOD SITUATION IN MAJOR RIVERS:

    River flows are expected to increase significantly in all the major rivers due to the expected precipitation from 20th July 2026 onward. However, flows in the Ravi and Sutlej rivers will also depend on releases from upstream Indian reservoir.
