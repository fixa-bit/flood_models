RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I. METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • Yesterday's trough of westerly wave over northwest Iran today lies over northeast Iran.

  • Seasonal low-pressure area lies over northeast Balochistan.

  • Yesterday's low pressure system over northeast Madhya Pradesh has moved slightly westwards and today lies over central Madhya Pradesh (India). 

  • Weak moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into the upper parts of the country up to 3,000 feet.

II. METEOROLOGICAL OUTLOOK              

  • Mainly hot and humid weather is expected over most parts of the country, however, Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of Sutlej and Ravi rivers.

 

III. HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST

  • Tarbela reservoir is currently at its Maximum Conservation Level, while Mangla reservoir is 75% filled.  

  • River Indus at Guddu is at Medium Flood Level while Taunsa and Sukkur is at Low Flood Level (downstream).

     

IV. HYDROLOGICAL OUTLOOK

  • River Indus at Guddu is likely to remain Medium Flood Level (upstream) within next 24-hours.

V. WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Mainly hot and humid weather is expected over most parts of the country with increasing trend in temperatures, however, Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of Sutlej and Ravi rivers.

VI. WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS   

  • Mainly hot and humid weather is expected over most parts of the country, however, Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of Sutlej, Ravi and Chenab rivers, Kashmir,

    Punjab: Narowal and Sialkot.

WEEKLY SIGNIFICANT OUTLOOK 

(28th August, 2026 to 03rd  September, 2026)

I. RAINFALL OUTLOOK

  • A monsoon trough is expected to affect upper catchments of all the major rivers moderately from 29th August to 02nd September. Consequently, scattered to widespread rainfall activity is expected in the upper catchments of all the major rivers during the period.

II. FLOOD OUTLOOK

  • On account of expected monsoon trough from 29th August to 02nd September, flows in rivers Jhelum, Chenab and Ravi along with their associated nullahs are expected to increase moderately during the period.

5. HOW CLOSE IS EACH RIVER TO ITS LIMIT?

Guddu is in medium flood at 388.5 — 111.5 below its high flood level (500.0). Each bar is one station’s own flood levels across its maximum capacity; the solid run is the flow so far, the pale run is the room still left.

All figures in thousands of cusecs. Full bar = maximum (design) capacity. Solid colour is the flow reached so far and the pale wash beside it is the room still left; the dark line between them is the current reading, printed beneath it. Thin white lines are that station’s flood levels with their thresholds beneath, so the same colour can begin at a different point on every bar. Each band is named inside itself — initialled (BL, L, M, H, VH, EH) where the band is too narrow for the words, and left blank where it is too narrow for those. Stations without a design capacity are not shown.
