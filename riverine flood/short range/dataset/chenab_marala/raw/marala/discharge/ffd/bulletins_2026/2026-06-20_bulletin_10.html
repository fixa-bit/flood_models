DAILY FLOOD BULLETIN

  1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

 

I:  HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK  

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows.

 

     II:   METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES  

  1. A shallow trough of westerly wave lies over northern parts of Afghanistan. 
  2. Weak moist currents from the Arabian Sea are penetrating into the lower half of the country up to 3000 ft. 
  3. Seasonal low lies over Northwest Balochistan. 

 

III: WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Moderate rain/wind-thunderstorm is likely at isolated places over the upper catchments of the Indus River along with all districts of Gilgit-Baltistan.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Chitral, Upper Dir, Lower Dir, Swat, Shangla, Buner, Bajaur, Abbottabad, and Haripur.

Punjab: Multan, Lodhran, Khanewal, Bahawalpur, Bahawalnagar, Dera Ghazi Khan, and Rajanpur.

 

IV: WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS 

  • Rain/wind-thunderstorm is expected at isolated places over the upper catchments of the Indus River along with all districts of Gilgit-Baltistan.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Chitral, Upper Dir, Lower Dir, Swat, Shangla, Buner, Bajaur, Abbottabad, and Haripur.

Punjab: Multan, Lodhran, Khanewal, Bahawalpur, Bahawalnagar, Dera Ghazi Khan, and Rajanpur.

 

  1. WEEKLY SIGNIFICANT WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR RIVER’S CATCHMENTS

 

(21st-June, 2026 to 27th -June, 2026)

 

  • RAINFALL OUTLOOK:

No flood generating rainfall is expected over upper catchments of all Major Rivers. 

  • FLOOD SITUATION IN MAJOR RIVERS:

No flood situation is expected.
