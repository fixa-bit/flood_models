RAINFALL FORECAST — NEXT 24 & 48 HOURS

1.

RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I:

HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows.

  • ALERT: On account of an approaching westerly wave coupled with a strong monsoon incursion from 20th to 23rd July over the catchments of all major rivers, flows are likely to increase significantly. 

  • Flash flooding is expected in the hill torrents of Dera Ghazi Khan, the streams of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and northern Balochistan. Urban Flooding is also expected in Rawalpindi, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Narowal, Lahore and Faisalabad. 

  • Medium to High Flood level is expected in River Chenab at Marala, Jhelum (upstream Mangla) and associated nullah of Jhelum, Chenab and Ravi. High flows are also expected in river Kabul and its associated tributaries.

II:

METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES

  • Yesterday’s trough of westerly wave over Northern parts of Iran has moved eastwards and today lies over northeast Iran.

  • Yesterday's low-pressure area over southeast Jharkhand (India), has moved eastwards and today lies over central Jharkhand with central pressure 998 hPa. It is expected to move westwards and reach northwest Jharkhand during the next 24 hours.

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northwest Balochistan.

  • Weak moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into the central parts of the country up to 3000 feet.

III:

WEATHER FORECAST FOR NEXT 24-HOURS

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over upper catchments of rivers Sutlej, Ravi and Chenab & Kashmir.

  • Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over Islamabad

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Bajaur, Buner, Charsadda, Chitral, Haripur, Khyber, Kurram, Malakand, Mansehra, Mardan, Mohmand, Nowshera, Peshawar, Shangla, Swabi and Swat.

    Punjab: Rawalpindi, Attock, Gujrat, Jhelum, Narowal, and Sialkot.

IV:

WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS

Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over upper catchments of all major rivers along with Islamabad, Kashmir,

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Bajaur, Batagram, Buner, Charsadda, Haripur, Khyber, Kurram, Mardan, Mohmand, Nowshera, Orakzai, Peshawar, Shangla, Swabi, Swat and Dir.

Punjab: Rawalpindi, Attock, Chakwal, Gujranwala, Gujrat, Hafizabad, Jhelum, Lahore, Mandi Bahauddin, Narowal, Sheikhupura and Sialkot.

Balochistan: Khuzdar, Musakhel and Zhob.

2.

WEEKLY WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR RIVER’S CATCHMENTS

(18th- July, 2026 to 24th- July, 2026)

RAINFALL OUTLOOK:

ALERT:Widespread thunderstorm/rain with isolated heavy to very heavy falls/torrential rain is expected over upper catchments of all the major rivers along with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit Baltistan, Kashmir and upper Punjab from 20th July 2026. 

FLOOD SITUATION IN MAJOR RIVERS:

ALERT: River flows are expected to increase significantly in all the major rivers due to the expected precipitation from 20th July 2026 onward. However, flows in the Ravi and Sutlej rivers will also depend on releases from upstream Indian reservoirs.
