RAINFALL FORECAST — NEXT 24 & 48 HOURS

1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I: HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK

  • River Ravi at Sidhnai is in Medium flood level. River Indus at Kalabagh, Chashma, Taunsa and Guddu, river Chenab at Khanki, Qadirabad and Trimmu are in Low flood level. All major rivers at other gauging stations are flowing in their normal flows.

  • River Indus at Guddu is expected to attain Medium flood level, whereas, Sukkur will attain Low flood level in next 24 to 36 hours.

II: METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES

  • A fresh trough of westerly wave lies over northwest Afghanistan.

  • Yesterday's low-pressure area over west Rajasthan (India) has become insignificant. 

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northwest Balochistan.

  • Weak moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into the country up to 3,000 feet.

  • Yesterday’s low pressure over south of West Bengal (India) and adjoining Orissa (India) remained stationary and has become well-marked low (996 hPa). It is likely to move northwestwards towards northeast Orissa and adjoining Jharkand (India).

III: WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchment of all major rivers, Islamabad, Kashmir, 

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Abbottabad, Batagram, Buner, Haripur, Kohistan, Mansehra, Mardan, Shangla, Swabi, Swat and Tor Ghar.

    Gilgit-Baltistan: Astore, Darel, Diamir, Gilgit and Tangir.

5. HOW CLOSE IS EACH RIVER TO ITS LIMIT?

Sidhnai is in low to medium flood at 60.6 — 29.4 below its very high flood level (90.0). Each bar is one station’s own flood levels across its maximum capacity; the solid run is the flow so far, the pale run is the room still left.

All figures in thousands of cusecs. Full bar = maximum (design) capacity. Solid colour is the flow reached so far and the pale wash beside it is the room still left; the dark line between them is the current reading, printed beneath it. Thin white lines are that station’s flood levels with their thresholds beneath, so the same colour can begin at a different point on every bar. Each band is named inside itself — initialled (BL, L, M, H, VH, EH) where the band is too narrow for the words, and left blank where it is too narrow for those. Stations without a design capacity are not shown.
