RAINFALL FORECAST — NEXT 24 & 48 HOURS

1.

RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I:

HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows, however, low to medium flood level is expected in river Chenab at Marala, Ravi at Jassar within next 24-hours.

  • WARNING: On account of an approaching westerly wave coupled with a strong monsoon incursion from 20th to 24th  July over the catchments of all major rivers, flows are likely to increase significantly. 

    • High Flood level is expected in River Chenab at Marala, Jhelum at upstream Mangla and associated nullahs of Jhelum, Chenab (Dora, Dotta, Dowara, Bhimber, Halsi, Aik and Palkhu) and Ravi (Bein, Bassanter, Deg and Sakki). 

    • High flows are also expected in river Kabul and its associated tributaries.

    • Flash flooding is expected in the hill torrents of DG Khan, the streams of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and northern Balochistan.

    • Urban Flooding is also expected in Rawalpindi, Jhelum, Gujranwala, Sargodha, Khushab, Sialkot, Gujrat, Narowal, Lahore, Okara, Sahiwal, Bahawalnagar and Faisalabad. 

II:

METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES

  • Yesterday’s trough of westerly wave over northeast Iran today lies over Afghanistan.

  • Yesterday's low-pressure area over central Jharkhand (India) has moved northeastwards and today lies over northwest Jharkhand (India).

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northwest Balochistan.

  • Moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into the central parts and upper parts of the country up to 5000 feet.

QUANTITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST OF GAUGING STATIONS (IN THOUSANDS OF CUSECS)

% of Capacity = inflow as a share of the station’s design capacity. It is the capacity left before the channel is overwhelmed.

Spatial: Isolated=20 to 30% of area | Scattered=31 to 50% of area | Fairly Widespread=51 to 75% of area | Widespread=76 to 100% of area, R= Rising, F=Falling

Intensity (mm/24 hours): Light ≤ 10, Moderate =10.1 – 30, Heavy=30.1 – 70, Very Heavy=70.1 - 150, Extremely Heavy > 150
