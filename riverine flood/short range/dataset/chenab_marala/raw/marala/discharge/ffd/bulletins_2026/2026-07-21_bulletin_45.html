RAINFALL FORECAST — NEXT 24 & 48 HOURS

1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I: HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK

  • River Indus at Kalabagh, River Chenab at Khanki are in Low flood level. All other major rivers are in their normal flow.

  • WARNING: In the presence of a westerly weather system coupled with strong monsoon incursions from the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal, Fairly widespread to widespread rain-wind/thunderstorm with isolated heavy to very heavy falls is expected over the catchments of all major rivers, including upper Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Consequently, river flows are likely to increase significantly upto 24th July.

    • High Flood level is expected in River Chenab at Marala, Jhelum at upstream Mangla and associated nullahs of Jhelum, Chenab (Dora, Dotta, Dowara, Bhimber, Halsi, Aik and Palkhu) and Ravi (Bein, Bassanter, Deg and Sakki). 

    • High flows are also expected in river Kabul and its associated tributaries.

    • Flash flooding is expected in the hill torrents of DG Khan, the streams of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and northern Balochistan.

    • Urban Flooding is also expected in Rawalpindi, Jhelum, Gujranwala, Sargodha, Mianwali, Mandi Bahauddin, Khushab, Sialkot, Gujrat, Narowal, Lahore, Okara, Sahiwal and Faisalabad. 

    •  

II: METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES

  • Yesterday’s trough of westerly wave over northern parts of the country persists.

  • Monsoon trough extends from northeastern parts of the country passes through northern parts of India along the foot hills of Himalaya.

  • Yesterday's low-pressure area over south Utter Pradesh (India) has moved westwards and today lies as upper air cyclonic circulation at southwest Utter Pradesh (India).

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over Northwest Balochistan.

  • Moist currents from Arabian sea and Bay of Bengal are penetrating into the central and upper parts of the country up to 5000 feet.
