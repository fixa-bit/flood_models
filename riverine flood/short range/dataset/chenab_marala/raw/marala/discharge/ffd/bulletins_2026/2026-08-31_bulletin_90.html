RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I. METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • Yesterday's trough of westerly wave over north Afghanistan, today lies over northeast Afghanistan.

  • Seasonal low-pressure area lies over northeast Balochistan.

  • Yesterday's low pressure system over north Bay of Bengal and adjoining northeast Orrisa (India) persists.

  • Moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into the upper parts of the country up to 3,000 feet.

II. METEOROLOGICAL OUTLOOK              

  • Scattered to widespread rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of  all the major rivers, Islamabad, Kashmir, upper Punjab and Upper Khyber Pakhtunkhwa during 31st August to 02nd September. However, isolated heavy fall is also expected over the upper catchments of Indus, Jhelum and Chenab rivers during the period.

III. HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST

  • Tarbela reservoir is currently at its Maximum Conservation Level, while Mangla reservoir is 76% filled.  

  • All major rivers are flowing at normal levels.

IV. HYDROLOGICAL OUTLOOK

  • Flows in rivers Jhelum and Chenab and their associated tributaries are expected to increase moderately during next 48-hours.

V. WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Widespread rain-wind/thunderstorm with isolated heavy fall is expected over the upper catchments of Indus, Jhelum and Chenab rivers, Islamabad, Kashmir,

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Buner, Haripur, Mardan and Swabi. 

    Punjab: Attock, Gujrat, Jhelum, Narowal, Rawalpindi and Sialkot.

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of Ravi and Sutlej rivers, 

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Abbottabad, Bajaur, Batagram, Charsadda, Hangu, Karak, Khyber, Kohat, Kohistan, Kurram, Malakand, Mansehra, Mohmand, Nowshera, Orakzai, Peshawar, Shangla, Swat, Tor Ghar and Dir. 

    Punjab: Chakwal, Gujranwala, Hafizabad, Lahore, Mandi Bahauddin, Sargodha and Sheikhupura. 

  • Mainly hot and humid weather is expected over remaining most parts of the country. 

VI. WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS   

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of all the major rivers, Islamabad & Kashmir. However, isolated heavy falls is also expected over the upper catchments of Sutlej, Ravi and Chenab rivers.

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Abbottabad, Bajaur, Bannu, Batagram, Buner, Charsadda, Hangu, Haripur, Karak, Khyber, Kohat, Kohistan, Kurram, Lakki Marwat, Malakand, Mansehra, Mardan, Mohmand, Waziristan, Nowshera, Orakzai, Peshawar, Shangla, Swabi, Swat, Tor Ghar and Dir.

  • Mainly hot and humid weather is expected over remaining most parts of the country.

5. HOW CLOSE IS EACH RIVER TO ITS LIMIT?

Kalabagh is in low flood at 253.0 — 122.0 below its medium flood level (375.0). Each bar is one station’s own flood levels across its maximum capacity; the solid run is the flow so far, the pale run is the room still left.

All figures in thousands of cusecs. Full bar = maximum (design) capacity. Solid colour is the flow reached so far and the pale wash beside it is the room still left; the dark line between them is the current reading, printed beneath it. Thin white lines are that station’s flood levels with their thresholds beneath, so the same colour can begin at a different point on every bar. Each band is named inside itself — initialled (BL, L, M, H, VH, EH) where the band is too narrow for the words, and left blank where it is too narrow for those. Stations without a design capacity are not shown.
