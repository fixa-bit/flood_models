DAILY FLOOD BULLETIN

  1. RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I:    HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST AND OUTLOOK            

  • All the major rivers are flowing within their normal flows.

 

     II:  METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • A fresh trough of westerly wave lies over northern parts of Iran.

  • The seasonal low-pressure area lies over northwest Balochistan.

  • Yesterday's low-pressure area over North Madhya Pradesh (India) has moved slightly westwards and today lies over Northeast Rajasthan, with a central pressure of 995 hPa. It is expected to move northeastward, reach the central parts of Uttar Pradesh (India), and weaken further during the next 24 hours.

  • Weak moist currents from Arabian sea and Bay of Bengal are penetrating upper parts of the country up to 3000 feet.

 

III:  WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of all the major rivers, Islamabad and Kashmir. 

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Abbottabad, Buner, Dir, Kohistan, Mansehra and Swat. 

Punjab: Rawalpindi, Jhelum, Gujrat, Narowal, Sialkot and DG Khan. 

  • Hot and humid weather is expected for rest of the country.

 

IV: WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS 

No significant change.

 

  1. WEEKLY WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR RIVER’S CATCHMENTS

(10th- July, 2026 to 16th- July, 2026)

 

  • RAINFALL OUTLOOK:

    Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm with isolated heavy falls is expected over the upper catchments of all major rivers, Islamabad, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Kashmir, Gilgit Baltistan and North Punjab from 11th to 13th July.

     

  • FLOOD SITUATION IN MAJOR RIVERS:

    Flows in all the major rivers are expected to slightly increase from 11th to 13th July.
