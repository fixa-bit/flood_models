RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I. METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • Yesterday's trough of westerly wave over northeast Afghanistan and adjoining Pakistan persists.

  • Yesterday's low pressure system over north Madhya Pradesh (India) today lies over northwest Madhya Pradesh (India) with central pressure 999 hPa. 

  • A fresh low pressure system has developed over south West Bengal (India) with central pressure 999 hPa. 

  • Seasonal low-pressure area lies over north Balochistan.

  • Weak moist currents from Arabian sea and Bay of Bengal are penetrating into the upper parts of the country up to 3,000 feet.

II. METEOROLOGICAL OUTLOOK              

  • Scattered rain-wind/thunderstorm with isolated heavy falls is expected over the upper catchments of eastern rivers including Islamabad, Kashmir and upper Punjab during next 24-hours. 

 

III. HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST

  • Tarbela reservoir is currently at its Maximum Conservation Level and is at full capacity, while Mangla reservoir is 66% filled.  

  • River Indus at Kalabagh and Guddu is in Low flood level at downstream.

     

IV. HYDROLOGICAL OUTLOOK

  • Flows in all major rivers are expected to remain in the normal flow limits. However river Ravi and Chenab may attain Low Flood Level. Moreover, the nullahs of river Ravi and Chenab can also attain low to medium flood level during next 24 hours.

5. HOW CLOSE IS EACH RIVER TO ITS LIMIT?

Kalabagh is in low flood at 288.5 — 86.5 below its medium flood level (375.0). Each bar is one station’s own flood levels across its maximum capacity; the solid run is the flow so far, the pale run is the room still left.

All figures in thousands of cusecs. Full bar = maximum (design) capacity. Solid colour is the flow reached so far and the pale wash beside it is the room still left; the dark line between them is the current reading, printed beneath it. Thin white lines are that station’s flood levels with their thresholds beneath, so the same colour can begin at a different point on every bar. Each band is named inside itself — initialled (BL, L, M, H, VH, EH) where the band is too narrow for the words, and left blank where it is too narrow for those. Stations without a design capacity are not shown.
