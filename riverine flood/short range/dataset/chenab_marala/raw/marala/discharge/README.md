# Marala discharge sources

The [combined Marala discharge catalog](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_discharge_all_sources.csv) preserves every collected value as a separate row with its date, discharge in cusecs, source URL, local source file, and Pakistan observation time **where confirmed**. Its [report](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_discharge_all_sources_report.json) counts 3,988 rows: 3,216 WAPDA inflows, 363 FFD inflows, 363 FFD outflows, 30 PMD historical event peaks, and eight IRSA daily-mean inflows plus eight daily-mean outflows. There are 727 rows with a confirmed time and 3,261 with the time left blank. `record_kind` separates dated observations, daily means, and selected event peaks. Overlapping sources remain separate, and gaps are not filled.

```text
discharge/
|-- wapda/   # original WAPDA historical and daily-report workbooks
|-- ffd/     # original FFD bulletin PDFs (2024-25) and HTML pages (2026)
|-- pmd/     # other PMD publications
`-- irsa/    # separate daily-mean reports and source-check evidence
```

FFD is part of PMD, but its bulletins are stored separately to preserve their source. The model's `processed/marala/discharge/` folder contains extracted tables; original publications remain here.

**WAPDA:** The [River Flow page](https://wapda.gov.pk/river-flow/) links the downloaded [historical workbook](https://wapda.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/GRAPH-DG-16-for-MAIL-2.xls). Its Marala inflow column yielded [3,216 daily values](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_wapda_daily_inflow.csv), 1 March 2016 to 19 December 2024. The [extraction report](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_wapda_extraction_report.json) flags the zero on 23 January 2021 for review. The workbook gives dates but **does not state a time for each historical row**. A [WAPDA report for 19 December 2024](https://wapda.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Daily-DataPress-Release-19-12-2024.xls) explicitly labels that day's Marala reading at 0600 hours, and its 5,800 cusecs matches the historical workbook. Only that WAPDA date is marked time-confirmed in the catalog; the other historical times remain blank.

**FFD:** The [bulletin archive](https://ffd.pmd.gov.pk/bulletins/archive) and [daily bulletins](https://ffd.pmd.gov.pk/bulletin/) supplied 2024 and 2025 PDF bulletins and 2026 HTML bulletins. Extracted Marala inflow and outflow are in the [2024](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_ffd_2024_bulletin_discharge.csv), [2025](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_ffd_2025_bulletin_discharge.csv), and [2026](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_ffd_2026_bulletin_discharge.csv) tables. All 363 extracted bulletins explicitly label observations at **06:00 Pakistan time**. Each table row retains the source URL and source-file checksum. The [2024 report](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_ffd_2024_collection_report.json) has 122 extracted bulletins from 16 June to 15 October (120 unique printed dates); the [2025 report](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_ffd_2025_collection_report.json) has 119 from 15 June to 15 October (117 unique printed dates); the [2026 report](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_ffd_2026_collection_report.json) has 122 consecutive dates from 16 June to 15 October. These are flood-season bulletins, not year-round records.

The archive listing and the date printed in some 2024/2025 PDFs disagree. The tables keep both dates and flag the mismatch; the combined catalog uses the printed date and carries the flag. Within the printed-date spans, 2024 lacks 2 September and 3 October; 2025 lacks 30 June, 6 August, 1-2 September, 4 October, and 12 October. Both years also contain duplicate printed dates; see their reports. Several 2024 bulletin inflows disagree with WAPDA for the same printed date and are flagged in the combined catalog. Resolve these source conflicts before building a training series.

**Other PMD publication:** A [Pakistan Journal of Meteorology paper](https://www.pmd.gov.pk/rnd/rndweb/rnd_new/journal/vol13_issue26_files/5_Flood_Forecasting_of_an_Ungauged_Trans-boundary_Chenab_River_Basin_Using_Distributed_Hydrological_Model_Integrated_Flood_Analysis_System_%28IFAS%29.pdf) tabulates [30 selected Marala flood peaks](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_pmd_historical_flood_peaks.csv), dated 1970-2016. They are event maxima without a stated observation time, not daily observations; do not append them to a daily training series.

**Prepared baseline:** The [daily WAPDA inflow table](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_daily_inflow_clean.csv) retains 3,216 dates and withholds seven unresolved values from modeling columns. The [preparation notes](../../../processed/marala/discharge/README.md) explain the review, the recent-source search and remaining gaps.

**IRSA:** The [daily-data page](http://pakirsa.gov.pk/DailyData.aspx) supplied eight daily-mean Marala reports for 16-23 October 2026, saved under `irsa/`. Their [extracted table](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_irsa_daily_mean_discharge.csv) remains separate from 06:00 point readings. Full year-round 2025-2026 access was not verified; see the [search evidence](../../../processed/marala/discharge/marala_recent_source_search_report.json).
